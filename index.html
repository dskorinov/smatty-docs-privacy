Smatty Patty

com.smattypatty.smatty_patty · Last updated: August 17, 2026

This privacy policy explains how the Smatty Patty mobile application ("the App") handles user data.

Summary

The App does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. All data used by the App stays on your device and is never sent to any server operated by us or any third party.

Data collection

We do not collect any of the following:

  • Personal information (name, email, phone number, etc.)
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Location data
  • Usage analytics or crash reports
  • Photos, video or audio
  • Any data transmitted to external servers

Camera

The App includes a QR code tool. When you open its scanner, the App asks for permission to use the camera so that it can read a QR code you point it at.

  • The camera image is analysed on your device, in the moment, only to find a QR code in it.
  • No photo or video is taken, saved, or stored anywhere.
  • Nothing from the camera is transmitted off your device, to us or to anyone else.
  • The text of a scanned code is shown on screen and is not kept after you leave the tool, unless you copy or share it yourself.
  • The permission is requested only when you open the scanner, and the App works without it — every other tool is unaffected if you decline.

Third-party services

The App does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or data-tracking services.

Accounts and login

The App does not require registration or login, and does not store any account information.

Data storage

Any data the App uses (such as settings or app state) is stored locally on your device only and is not accessible to us.

Children's privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13, as it collects no data at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes (for example, if the App later adds features that involve data collection), this section will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will reflect the change.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, you can contact the developer at: smatty-support-group@googlegroups.com

Smatty Docs

com.smattypatty.smatty_docs · Last updated: October 29, 2026

This privacy policy explains how the Smatty Docs mobile application ("the App") handles user data.

Summary

The App does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. The documents you add, the text taken from them, the search index built from that text and the answers you get are all created and kept on your device, and are never sent to any server operated by us or by anyone else. The App itself makes exactly one kind of network request: downloading a language model file that you have chosen, described under Downloading a model below. The document scanner it opens is provided by Google Play services and sends Google diagnostic data, but never your pages; see Scanning paper documents.

Your documents

The App exists to answer questions about files you choose to add to it. Those files are handled as follows:

  • A document you add is copied into the App's own private storage area on your device. Other apps on the phone cannot read that area.
  • The copy is encrypted at rest, with a key held by the Android keystore on your device.
  • The text extracted from a document, the search index built from it, and your chat history are stored the same way, in the same place.
  • Nothing in that storage is uploaded, backed up to our servers, or shared with any third party — because there are no servers and no third parties.
  • Deleting a document in the App deletes the file, its text and its index entries together. Uninstalling the App removes all of it.

The App reads a file only at the moment you pick it or finish a scan, and only that file. It does not scan your device for other documents, photos or media.

Backups and device transfer

The App is excluded from Android's automatic cloud backup and from device-to-device transfer. Your library therefore does not travel to Google's backup servers or to a new phone by those routes. This also means that if you lose the device or uninstall the App, the library is gone — keep your own copies of anything important.

Data collection

We do not collect any of the following:

  • Personal information (name, email, phone number, etc.)
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Location data
  • Usage analytics or crash reports (Google's document scanner sends Google its own diagnostics; see below)
  • Photos, video or audio
  • The content of your documents, or any question you ask about them
  • Any data transmitted to external servers — we operate none

Scanning paper documents

The Scan tab opens Google's document scanner, part of Google Play services (ML Kit). It finds the edges of each page in the camera picture, takes the picture, crops it, and hands the App one PDF of all the pages. That PDF is then treated like any document you add: encrypted into the App's private storage, and the scanner's temporary copy is deleted.

  • The pictures are processed on your device. Neither the App nor the scanner uploads them.
  • The camera is used through Google Play services' own camera permission; the App does not ask for one.
  • The first time it is used, Google Play services may download the scanner itself.
  • ML Kit sends Google diagnostic and usage data: device information (manufacturer, model, Android version), the App's package name and version, a per-installation identifier that is not meant to identify you or your device, performance figures, scanner settings, error codes and the sizes of input and output. Google states this is encrypted in transit, used for diagnostics and usage analytics, and not shared with third parties. It contains no picture, page or text from your documents. Google's privacy policy governs it.

Answers to your questions

Questions you ask are answered by a language model that runs on your device. The question and the passages from your documents that are used to answer it stay on the device throughout.

The App does not have an option to send your documents or questions to an online service. If such an option is ever added, it will be off by default, will require your explicit consent before anything is sent, will send only the passages relevant to the question rather than whole documents, and this section will be updated to say exactly what is sent and to whom before that version is published.

Downloading a model

No language model is included in the App, so before it can answer anything you choose one and download it. This is the one time the App makes a network request, and it is worth describing exactly:

  • Model files are downloaded from Hugging Face (huggingface.co), a third-party hosting service, at your request and to your choice of model.
  • Your device therefore contacts Hugging Face directly. As with any download, Hugging Face can see your IP address and which file you asked for. We do not receive that information, and we do not operate a server involved in it.
  • Nothing about you is attached to the request. No account, no identifier, and nothing from your documents.
  • If you enter a Hugging Face access token — needed only for models whose publisher restricts access — it is stored in the Android keystore on your device and sent only to Hugging Face, to authenticate that download.
  • Downloaded models are stored in the App's own private storage alongside your documents, and are deleted when you delete them or uninstall the App.

Hugging Face's own privacy policy governs what they do with a request their servers receive. If you would rather not contact them at all, the App simply has no model and cannot answer questions; everything else in it still works.

Permissions

The App asks for no runtime permissions. Files are added through the Android system file picker, which hands the App the single file you chose and grants no access to anything else. Scanning uses Google Play services' camera permission, not one of the App's.

Third-party services

The App does not integrate any analytics, advertising, or data-tracking services of its own. The third parties involved are Hugging Face, when you download a model, and Google, whose document scanner sends the diagnostics described above when you scan.

Accounts and login

The App does not require registration or login, and does not store any account information.

Children's privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13. The only data that leaves the device is the scanner diagnostics described above, which Google receives and which contain nothing from your documents.

What this policy does not promise

The protections above are aimed at other apps on your phone and at data leaving the device by accident. They are not designed to defend a device that has been rooted or compromised, and they do not stop someone who is holding your phone while it is unlocked from opening the App. Locking your device with a PIN, pattern or biometrics is what protects against that.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes — for example, if the App later adds a feature that involves sending data anywhere — this section will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will reflect the change.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, you can contact the developer at: smatty-support-group@googlegroups.com
