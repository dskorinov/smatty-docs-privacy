Privacy Policy for Smatty Docs

Last updated: August 19, 2026

This privacy policy explains how the Smatty Docs mobile application ("the App") handles user data.

Summary

The App does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. The documents you add, the text taken from them, the search index built from that text and the answers you get are all created and kept on your device, and are never sent to any server operated by us or by anyone else. The App makes exactly one kind of network request: downloading a language model file that you have chosen, described under Downloading a model below.

Your documents

The App exists to answer questions about files you choose to add to it. Those files are handled as follows:

  • A document you add is copied into the App's own private storage area on your device. Other apps on the phone cannot read that area.
  • The copy is encrypted at rest, with a key held by the Android keystore on your device.
  • The text extracted from a document, the search index built from it, and your chat history are stored the same way, in the same place.
  • Nothing in that storage is uploaded, backed up to our servers, or shared with any third party — because there are no servers and no third parties.
  • Deleting a document in the App deletes the file, its text and its index entries together. Uninstalling the App removes all of it.

The App reads a file only at the moment you pick it, and only the file you picked. It does not scan your device for other documents, photos or media.

Backups and device transfer

The App is excluded from Android's automatic cloud backup and from device-to-device transfer. Your library therefore does not travel to Google's backup servers or to a new phone by those routes. This also means that if you lose the device or uninstall the App, the library is gone — keep your own copies of anything important.

Data collection

We do not collect any of the following:

  • Personal information (name, email, phone number, etc.)
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Location data
  • Usage analytics or crash reports
  • Photos, video or audio
  • The content of your documents, or any question you ask about them
  • Any data transmitted to external servers — we operate none

Answers to your questions

Questions you ask are answered by a language model that runs on your device. The question and the passages from your documents that are used to answer it stay on the device throughout.

The App does not have an option to send your documents or questions to an online service. If such an option is ever added, it will be off by default, will require your explicit consent before anything is sent, will send only the passages relevant to the question rather than whole documents, and this page will be updated to say exactly what is sent and to whom before that version is published.

Downloading a model

No language model is included in the App, so before it can answer anything you choose one and download it. This is the one time the App makes a network request, and it is worth describing exactly:

  • Model files are downloaded from Hugging Face (huggingface.co), a third-party hosting service, at your request and to your choice of model.
  • Your device therefore contacts Hugging Face directly. As with any download, Hugging Face can see your IP address and which file you asked for. We do not receive that information, and we do not operate a server involved in it.
  • Nothing about you is attached to the request. No account, no identifier, and nothing from your documents.
  • If you enter a Hugging Face access token — needed only for models whose publisher restricts access — it is stored in the Android keystore on your device and sent only to Hugging Face, to authenticate that download.
  • Downloaded models are stored in the App's own private storage alongside your documents, and are deleted when you delete them or uninstall the App.

Hugging Face's own privacy policy governs what they do with a request their servers receive. If you would rather not contact them at all, the App simply has no model and cannot answer questions; everything else in it still works.

Permissions

The App asks for no runtime permissions. Files are added through the Android system file picker, which hands the App the single file you chose and grants no access to anything else.

Third-party services

The App does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or data-tracking services. The only third party it contacts at all is Hugging Face, when you download a model, as described above.

Accounts and login

The App does not require registration or login, and does not store any account information.

Children's privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13, as it collects no data at all.

What this policy does not promise

The protections above are aimed at other apps on your phone and at data leaving the device by accident. They are not designed to defend a device that has been rooted or compromised, and they do not stop someone who is holding your phone while it is unlocked from opening the App. Locking your device with a PIN, pattern or biometrics is what protects against that.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes — for example, if the App later adds a feature that involves sending data anywhere — this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will reflect the change.
